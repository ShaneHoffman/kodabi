the aesthetic, before the screens

Calm you notice before you notice why.

Kodabi records your meetings and quietly organizes them. It should feel unusually calm and beautiful — and never once announce a forest. The theme is an undertone, not a costume.

the bar

Linear and Things 3 — not an admin panel.

The reference isn't a productivity app wearing a nature skin. It's the class of software that feels considered in the hand: typography-first, chrome-last, fast, and quiet. Every screen clears that bar before it ships.

we hold to

  • Typography carries the hierarchy
  • Space instead of borders and boxes
  • One motion, used deliberately
  • Precision in the small things
  • Restraint as the default

we refuse

  • Dense data tables and grids
  • Boxes nested inside boxes
  • Motion and color used to shout
  • Chrome competing with content
  • Decorative theming

the four principles

principle間 · ma

Negative space is a material, not a leftover.

Ma is the charged interval between things. We compose with emptiness on purpose: rank comes from how much room something is given — not from rules, fills, badges, or borders. When a layout feels busy, the fix is almost always to remove something and add space.

principle森 · forest palette

A forest you feel, not a forest you see.

Quiet neutrals drawn from moss, fern, mist, stone, washi, and sumi ink — desaturated nearly to grey, so the mood reads as calm rather than green. Value carries the hierarchy; hue stays almost silent. Exactly one living green exists, and it is spent only on the listening state.

principle侘寂 · wabi-sabi

Quiet, warm, and unfinished on purpose.

Wabi-sabi prefers the plain and weathered to the glossy and complete. We favor warmth over polish, let a little asymmetry stand, and treat "done" as the point where removing one more thing would break it. Nothing is decorative; everything earns its place.

principlerestraint · guardrail

Evoke the archetype. Never trace it.

The theme is expressed by what we leave out. The kodama is suggested through calm, space, warmth, and a single green — never drawn. No leaf icons, no wood-grain, no spirit illustrations. If a choice makes the forest obvious, it's wrong.

typography · roles

One sans for the interface, one serif for reading.

The interface speaks in a humanist sans; long-form reading views switch to a warm serif. Hierarchy comes from scale, weight, and space — almost never from color. (Faces shown are system stand-ins; P0-4 chooses the real pair.)

references · mood plates

Five sources, rendered — not borrowed.

The moodboard is assembled from the named reference categories. Rather than paste in copyrighted photos, each is redrawn here as an original plate that keeps the discipline and drops the imagery — warmth and value, never literal nature.

Japanese stationery The discipline of a beautifully made blank page.
Ghibli backgrounds The light and air of the scene — never the characters.
Muji Everything unbranded, down to its function; anonymous quality.
Washi paper Warmth and tactile tooth, with no ornament.
Misty forest photography Depth built from value, not from detail — no trees drawn.

Original, generated art — the references are studied, never copied. Same principle the product follows: evoke the archetype, never trace it.

guardrail · theme as restraint

What we don't do.

The theme is expressed by what we leave out. Each temptation, and what we reach for instead.

  • Leaf & tree iconsspace and type

  • Spirit / mascot illustrationsa single breathing dot
  • Literal forest sceneryatmosphere from value
  • Color-coded UIhierarchy from value & spacing
  • Dense admin tablesgenerous, quiet rows

the one green

It appears only when kodabi is listening.

idle is absent · listening breathes

This is the entire budget for saturated color. Everywhere else the palette stays near-neutral; here, one quiet green marks that the app is recording — its consent signal and its logo in a single mark. The mark itself is designed in P0-5; here we only reserve the green and the feeling.

Indicative, not final — P0-4 locks the tokens and P0-5 designs the mark. The full written direction lives in DESIGN.md.
