the listening indicator is the kodama

One green. One breath.

The kodama is never drawn — no face, no leaf, no character. It is a single quiet mark: a still ink point when Kodabi is idle, and one living green that breathes when it is listening. The same mark is the logo, the consent signal, and the moment a screenshot sells.

two states · nothing in between to misread

Still when idle. Breathing when it listens.

The green is the entire budget for saturated color in the whole app — spent nowhere else. So its presence is unambiguous: green means Kodabi is recording, and its absence means it is not. That single reserved color is what lets one small mark carry consent.

Idle · not recording

A quiet ink point. Still, hue-silent, no aura. This is the resting logo — present, but plainly not listening.

Listening · recording

The one green arrives and the mark breathes inside a soft field of ma — a small living presence, attending.

the living mark · try it

Wake it, then let it hear a voice.

Waking warms the ink to green and draws the first breath. On top of that slow breath, a voice gently swells the aura — a whisper of a waveform, never a meter with bars. The envelope below is simulated; there is no microphone on this page.

Idle — a still ink mark. Press Wake to start listening.

animation intent · for P1-5

One breath, quietly modulated.

Two layered motions and one firm accessibility floor. Values here are indicative — a starting point the runtime ticket refines against real audio.

baseline

Breathing

A slow ~4.2s ease-in-out swell of the core and its aura — the resting rhythm of a presence that is simply listening. This runs whenever recording, even in silence.

overlay

Waveform whisper

Voice amplitude gently perturbs the aura's bloom — heavily smoothed and capped, so speech makes the breath swell rather than draw a scope. Never bars, never a level meter.

transition

Wake & settle

Idle→listening warms ink to green and blooms the aura over ~450ms; settling reverses it. One deliberate motion — the "it woke up" beat, not a flourish.

accessibility floor

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion every animation on this page stops and listening becomes a still green mark — unmistakably on-air by reserved color and presence alone. (Toggle it in your OS to see this page obey.)

The shipped app is gentler than this prototype: it stops every scale and rotation but keeps the aura's opacity breath and the starting pulse, because movement is what causes vestibular trouble and a fade is not. This page's own CSS was written before that call. See docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §4.

guardrail · evoke, never trace

What the mark is not.

The kodama archetype — a small, quiet forest presence — is suggested by calm, space, warmth, and one green. Every literal reading is refused.

  • A face, eyes, or a little bodya point and its breath
  • Ghibli's character, traced or softenedan original mark
  • Leaf, sprout, or tree glypha clean disc
  • A level meter or waveform scopea breath that swells
  • Green sprinkled through the UIone reserved green, listening only

Concept, not final — P0-5 designs the mark; P0-4 locks the exact green and geometry; P1-5 wires it to real capture state. The written concept lives in SPIRIT_MARK.md; the aesthetic it descends from is in DESIGN.md.
